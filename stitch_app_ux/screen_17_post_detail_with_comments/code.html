Best sunrise spots within 2 hours of the city

I've been mapping out all the accessible peaks and wanted to share my top 5 with you all. Each one has parking, is family-friendly, and offers incredible views:

  1. 1. Entoto Peak — the classic, never disappoints
  2. 2. Yerer Mountain — quieter, amazing eastern views
  3. 3. Furi Hill — closest to town, great for quick mornings
  4. 4. Wochecha Ridge — longer drive but worth every minute
  5. 5. Zikwala Crater — volcanic lake at sunrise is surreal
