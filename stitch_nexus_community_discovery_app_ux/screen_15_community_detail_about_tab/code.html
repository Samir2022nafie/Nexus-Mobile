About

Explore the great outdoors with like-minded adventurers. We organize weekly hikes, monthly camping trips, and nature photography walks. Whether you're a seasoned mountaineer or just starting to explore, there's a place for you here. Our community values Leave No Trace principles, mutual respect, and a love for nature.
